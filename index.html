Life & Legacy

Victoria A. Satorre was a dedicated educator who devoted much of her life to higher education in the Philippines. She served as Dean at the University of San Carlos in Cebu City — a Jesuit institution and one of the oldest universities in the Philippines — where she was recognized for her principled leadership and commitment to academic integrity. Her tenure included formal correspondence with the Philippine Ministry of Education, Culture and Sports, and her decisions as Dean were ultimately upheld by the Philippine Supreme Court.

Beyond her professional accomplishments, Victoria was a beloved wife, mother, and grandmother whose faith, warmth, and quiet strength shaped everyone she touched.
