A Family Tribute

Betty is remembered as a loving wife, mother, grandmother, sister, aunt, educator, and friend. A magna cum laude University of San Carlos accounting graduate, Central Michigan University MBA, Dean, and Miss Caltex, she carried intelligence and grace alongside deep Catholic faith, kindness, cooking, Mahjong, and joyful family gatherings.
